Help & How to Use

Overview

NorCal Trim Tools helps cannabis trimmers track daily grams, calculate bonus pay, and organize everything by bi-weekly pay periods — all in your browser with no login.

Your data is stored locally on your device (browser localStorage). You can export/import with CSV anytime.

Important: Use the Calculator and Trim Tracker in the same language version. Mixing languages can cause data-sync issues.

Using the Trim Calculator

  • Enter the total grams you trimmed for the day.
  • Optional: click "Add Today's Total from Trim Tracker" to auto-fill today’s grams (if you used the tracker today).
  • Click "Calculate" to see the bonus result on screen.
  • Click "Save to history" to actually save the entry into the table (session board).
Quick tip: If you only hit Calculate, nothing is saved. To add it to your session board, you must press Save to history.

Session Board (Table) & Pay Period Grouping

  • Your entries are grouped into bi-weekly pay periods (starting every other Monday).
  • The current pay period shows directly in the table.
  • Past pay periods are collapsed to keep the page clean.
  • Click the [+] to expand a past pay period and see the entries.
  • New: Each collapsed pay period header also shows the total bonus amount for that pay period in green.

Editing Entries

  • You can click and edit a date or grams value directly in the table.
  • If you edit grams, the bonus pay automatically recalculates.
  • If you edit a date, the entry will move to the correct pay period after saving.

Import / Export (CSV)

  • Export CSV downloads all your saved history as a file.
  • Import CSV lets you restore data from a backup (or move to a new phone/device).

Clearing Data

  • Clear Current Pay Period removes only entries from the active pay period.
  • Clear All Past Pay Periods deletes everything before the current pay period (current stays).
  • Always export a CSV first if you want a backup.

Hourly Pay Averages

  • Expand "Hourly Pay Averages ▼".
  • Enter your hourly rate.
  • Enter regular hours + minutes and overtime hours + minutes.
  • Choose a date range and click Calculate averages.
  • It totals your bonus from that range and combines it with hourly + overtime pay.

Using the Trim Tracker

  • Use the Trim Tracker to log grams per strain throughout the day.
  • It automatically totals your day and saves it with today’s date.
  • Back on the calculator, tap the green import button to pull today’s total into the grams input.

Need More Help?

If something looks wrong, refresh the page and double-check that your dates are in YYYY-MM-DD format.
